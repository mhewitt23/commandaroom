The method

Your voice tells the world who you are. When you speak, you lead.

But when you perform something instead of embodying it, your frame collapses internally and externally. A solid voice goes nowhere without a relevant message built on true confidence.

Asking yourself better questions is only part of the equation. There is also the courage and honesty with which you answer, and the urgency and accountability with which you take action on those answers. This, in addition to the coordination of your voice, is what creates your sound.

Voice, Thought, and Life: how the seven skills fit together

Voice.

How you sound.

Think of this as the fabric of your suit.

Resonance
A deeper, more commanding voice, without sounding like you’re trying too hard.
Articulation
Combined with Resonance, this signals competence and ensures every word is heard.
Imagination
Internal mapping of the coordination of sound production so you can sound how you want, when and where you want.

Thought.

What you say.

This is the cut of your suit.

Intention
Speaking to be validated makes you needy and self-conscious, which makes people act weird. Instead, speak only to help others win.
Subtext
The message embedded in your sound, without adding any extra words.

Life.

How you live.

Whether you wear the suit or the suit wears you.

Mindset
The transcription of the dialogue in your brain.
Stakes
The great clarifier: what happens if you don’t get the outcome you’re leading people towards?

A congruent message that is distinctly your own, that builds culture, anchors you in open-ended conversation, and filters your responses on the fly.

About

Michael Hewitt

I started coaching in 2015 as both a creative outlet and a way to make extra cash alongside my budding stage career. After I graduated top of my Master of Music class at Rice University and earned accolades and return engagements at leading operatic and musical theatre houses (WNO at The Kennedy Center, Arena Stage, Glimmerglass Festival), the coronavirus pandemic paused all stage work indefinitely.

I pivoted to cybersecurity sales, where I quickly found my stride as a full-cycle Account Executive, first as a pre-IPO hire at a sales-heavy organization and later as employee #36 at one struggling to find PMF.

After simultaneously being laid off and hitting rock bottom in my former marriage, I committed to building this practice, Command a Room, with the message that “your voice tells the world who you are” and the mission of helping people reclaim agency they’ve lost so they can do what they’re built to do.
