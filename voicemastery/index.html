Questions

Who is this for?

Professionals and executives who want to sound more authentic and feel less robotic or performative, warm yet firm and respectable. People who constantly lose their voice during a week of high-volume calls and conversations, or after a weekend of partying. People who hate their voice. People who want to sound hot and smart.

What if I just have a bad voice?

Nobody has an annoying or an ugly voice. It’s just not optimized. We train the instrument. We raise the floor, so that your average sound is more resonant and can do more things.

Will this make my voice deeper?

Deeper and more commanding, without sounding like you’re trying too hard. Low pitch alone is not authority.

Will I sound fake?

No. “Fake” means performative. We’re after authenticity and embodiment. Voice Mastery will eliminate any falsehood from your sound.
